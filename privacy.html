Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

"shorts-autopost" is a private, personal automation tool operated by its owner for their own YouTube channel. It has no public users.

What data the tool accesses

  • YouTube Data API: uploads videos to the owner's own channel and sets their titles and schedule.
  • Google Drive API (read-only): reads the list of video files in the owner's own Drive folder and downloads those files.

How the data is used and stored

  • OAuth credentials (client ID/secret and refresh token) are stored only in the encrypted Secrets storage of the owner's own private GitHub repository and are not shared with anyone.
  • No data is sold, shared, transferred to advertisers, or used for advertising purposes.
  • No analytics or tracking are used.
  • The tool does not collect any data about people who visit this page.
